# How it works

## Player and dialogs
- The Player section shows the disc's frame image, a spinning decorative disc, a condition label with words (Mint, Scratched, Skips), five real buttons and one line that reads out the month being played.
- The Details dialog (button or the I key) lists the dataset label and unit, its limits, colour match, frames used, the NASA SVS link and CREDITS.md; Esc closes it and focus goes back to where it was.
- Settings (High contrast, Reduce motion, Preview on focus, Volume) live in a dialog and are saved in this browser; contrast ratios of the colour pairs are checked by `node --test`.
