Made by team Earth Jukebox with help from Claude (SI) — see AI_USE.md. Data: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio (Earth Information Center).

Disc details

Piano roll

Dots: raindrops or data notes. Faint long bars: chord layers. Slashes over a whole month: missing data (crackle). Cut cell with a cross: skip.

Settings

Keys: Tab to a disc slot and press Enter to put it on the deck. Space play or pause, left and right arrows change month, F flip, M mode, I details, Esc stop.
