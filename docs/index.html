Where this month’s sound comes from
Learn the comparison sound

These short auditions use a fixed category scale and no accompaniment. They keep the same instrument, loudness and pitch rule for both places or years. The main record player keeps its landscape-inspired musical arrangements.

Rain: level −4 → E3, near normal → C4, level +4 → G♯4, moving two semitones per category. Greenness: categories 1–4 → C4, E4, G♯4, C5. More or greener means a higher note within that variable. Equal notes mean equal categories, not identical conditions.

Comparison: A plays first, then B, one second each. If either side is missing or has a flagged colour-match break, that pair is silent. A duet plays both available variables together for each month; the pitches of different variables are not interchangeable measurements.

The labelled controls work in portrait. Turn sideways for a larger record player.

Disc details

Piano roll

Dots: raindrops or data notes. Faint long bars: chord layers. Slashes over a whole month: missing data (crackle). Cut cell with a cross: skip.

Settings

Keys: Tab to a disc slot and press Enter to put it on the deck. Space play or pause, left and right arrows change month, F flip, M mode, I details, H how to use, Esc stop.
